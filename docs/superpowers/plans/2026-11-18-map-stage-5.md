# Map Stage 5 Implementation Plan: scenes, and sound and MIDI

**Goal:** Saved scenes switched with a fade (keys 1–9, the scene bar, MIDI or the beat), and a reactivity engine that lets sound, the beat, tempo and MIDI drive Map's shapes, instruments and scenes and Live's looks. Links are made with Learn or with ready-made reactions, and listed in one panel.

**Done when:**
- A three-scene show runs for two hours and switches without a black frame.
- Sound and a MIDI controller visibly drive a shape, an instrument setting, a scene change and a Live look.

**Architecture:** See "Stage 5 decisions" in the design.
- **`src/react/`** (new, shared by Map and Live):
  - pure rules: `features.ts`, `beat.ts`, `tempo.ts`, `links.ts`
  - inputs: `audio.ts`, `midi.ts`
  - the engine: `engine.ts`, in the studio window
- **Signals** go to the open output window in one small message a frame.
- **Map:**
  - the show moves to version 5 (scenes and links)
  - the compositor applies links each frame, for drawing only
  - triggers run in the studio window, as ordinary edits and switches
- **Live** applies its links to a copy of its settings each frame.

**Spec:** [docs/projection-mapping-design-2026-11-16.md](../../projection-mapping-design-2026-11-16.md): "Scenes", "Stage 5 decisions".

**Branch:** `map-stage-5` from `main` (f8eb6d3), in the `three VJ - main` folder.

**Execution:** native. Independent reviews:
- after Task 3 (format, scenes and links)
- after Task 6 (wall and Live applying signals)
- a whole-branch review at the end

## Global Constraints

- **Studio:** its loops, seeking and export must not change. Reactivity never touches a Studio project or its export.
- **The wall:** no black frame on a scene switch or any link change. A missing signal (no device, device unplugged) leaves every target at its set value.
- **Keeping things apart:**
  - Links change values for drawing only. The show and Live's settings keep the values the operator set.
  - Undo covers editing scenes and links, never signal movement.
- **Privacy:** the camera rule is unchanged. Sound is analysed in memory only, never recorded or sent anywhere. MIDI is input only, with no system-exclusive messages.
- **Limits:**
  - 64 links per show, and 64 in Live
  - 32 triggers
  - 32 scenes
  - the signals message under 16 KB
  - every existing limit still holds
- **Performance:** with eight shapes, four instruments and 16 links driven by sound, the measured run stays at the display's rate with no frame over 16.7 ms on a quiet Mac.

## Review Focus

1. **Scene switch with shared and changed instruments:**
   - instruments in both scenes keep running, with no re-prepare
   - an instrument only in the old scene is let go after the fade
   - the switch is never black

   Tests: Tasks 3 and 9.
2. **Deleting a shape, instrument or scene referred to by links, triggers or other scenes:** nothing dangles, and the file reopens unrepaired. Test: Task 3.
3. **Control-mode MIDI links:** Undo, saving, and reopening never bake the knob's value into the show. Test: Tasks 3 and 5.
4. **Audio device unplugged, or microphone permission refused:** told plainly, and targets return to their set values. Test: Task 2.
5. **Signals message size and rate:** bounded, and the wall ignores stale or malformed ones. Test: Task 4.

### Task 1: Pure rules (`src/react/`)

**Files:** `features.ts`, `beat.ts`, `tempo.ts`, `links.ts`, `signals.ts`. Tests: `tests/react-*.test.ts`.

- **`features`:** band energies from FFT magnitudes: bass 20–150 Hz, mids 150–2,000, highs 2,000–12,000, and level as RMS. Auto-gain is a running peak that rises fast and falls over about 8 s, with a noise floor, giving 0..1.
- **`beat`:** onsets from the rise in bass energy (spectral flux), with an adaptive threshold and a minimum gap of 0.25 s. The tempo is estimated from the gaps between onsets: a median folded into 70–180 BPM, needing 4 onsets before it is trusted. Output: a pulse that decays over 0.15 s, the beat count, and the BPM.
- **`tempo`:** one clock.
  - Its sources:
    - the detected beat
    - tap tempo: the average of the last 4 taps, reset after 2 s without one
    - MIDI clock: 24 ticks per beat, smoothed, with start and stop
    - a fixed BPM
  - It gives the phase per beat and per bar, and the beat count.
- **`signals`:** a snapshot type of numbers keyed by signal id, such as `audio.bass`, `beat.pulse`, `tempo.phase`, `midi.cc.1.74` and `midi.note.1.60`; validation; a size limit.
- **`links`:**
  - types for links and triggers
  - `evaluate(base, range, links, signals, smoothState, dt)`, which gives the drawn value, in range
  - smoothing (attack and release)
  - `fire(triggers, prev, now)`, which gives the actions, with edge detection (a note going on, a control rising past halfway, every N beats)

### Task 2: Inputs and the engine

**Files:**
- `src/react/audio.ts`: devices; start and stop; the analyser into features; unplug and refusal reported
- `src/react/midi.ts`: Web MIDI access; devices; control changes, notes and clock into signals; a learn listener; `inject(bytes)` for checks
- `src/react/engine.ts`: combines them a frame at a time; subscribers; device choices in localStorage; a test tone and injection for scripted checks
- `electron/main.cjs` and `electron/live.cjs`: the permission handler allows audio and MIDI (not system-exclusive) for the app's own windows, and macOS microphone access is asked for once

**Tests:** permission tables in `electron/live.test.cjs`, and the engine's pure parts.

### Task 3: Show version 5, with scenes and links

**Files:** `src/map/show.ts`, `src/map/editor.ts`, `src/map/scenes.ts` (pure). Tests: `tests/map-scenes.test.ts` and `tests/map-links.test.ts`.

- **Format:**
  ```ts
  Scene { id; name; fadeSeconds: number | null; shapes: Record<shapeId, SceneState> }
  SceneState = Pick<Shape, 'sourceId' | 'fill' | 'placement' | 'brightness' | 'tint' | 'softEdge' | 'border' | 'visible' | 'blocker'>
  ```
  - The show gains `scenes`, `sceneId`, `sceneFade`, `links` and `triggers`.
  - Each source gains `tempoSync`: null or a number of beats.
- **Scene edits:** `addScene` (takes in the current state), `renameScene`, `deleteScene`, `setSceneFade` and `switchScene`.
  - Switching writes the current shapes into the live scene, then applies the target scene.
  - Scene edits are undo steps.
- **Sources are kept while any scene uses them**, so `dropUnplayed` reads the scenes. Deleting a shape removes it from every scene, and from the links and triggers that name it.
- **Link and trigger edits:** `addLink`, `updateLink`, `removeLink`, `addTrigger`, `updateTrigger`, `removeTrigger` and `addReaction(preset, shapeId)`.
- **Validation:**
  - Every field is checked.
  - Links naming a missing target are dropped, and that is counted as a repair.
  - Version 1 to 4 files open unrepaired.

**Review after this task.**

### Task 4: Signals reach the wall

**Files:** `src/map/messages.ts` and `electron/map.cjs` (a `signals` message with its own size limit, in both copies), `src/ui/useMap.ts` (send once a frame while the output is open and reactivity is on), `src/map/wall.ts`.

### Task 5: The compositor applies links

**Files:** `src/map/compositor.ts`, `src/map/gpu.ts` (opacity in the shader), and `src/map/modulate.ts` (pure: turns a show, its links and the signals into what is drawn this frame).

- **Shapes:** brightness, opacity, zoom, turn, and the two moves.
- **Instruments:**
  - speed, and settings, changed on a copy of the layer parameters each frame
  - tempo sync advances the phase by the clock's beats
- **Scenes:** the scene's fade time is used for crossfades, and brightness and visibility glide.

### Task 6: Live applies links

**Files:** `src/live/settings.ts` (links in Live settings, validated), `src/live/LiveRenderer.ts` (signals and links applied to a copy of its settings each frame), and `electron/live.cjs` plus `src/ui/LiveOutput.tsx` (a `signals` message).

**Review after this task.**

### Task 7: Operator screens

**Files:** `src/ui/MapReactivity.tsx` (the panel), `src/ui/MapScenes.tsx` (the scene bar), `MapDetails.tsx` (a Reactions section and React to… on sliders), `LivePanel.tsx` (React to… and reactions), and the shared learn flow in `src/ui/react-learn.ts`.

- **The Reactivity panel:**
  - the device, with band meters
  - the MIDI devices, with a light that shows activity
  - the tempo source, BPM, and Tap (the T key)
  - links and triggers, each editable
- **The scene bar:**
  - one button per scene, labelled with its number and name
  - + to add a scene
  - fade time
  - right-click to rename, delete or set the scene's own fade
  - keys 1–9 switch scenes
- **Triggers:** added from the panel, with **Learn** for a note or control, or **Every N beats**.

### Task 8: Tempo sync and the reactions

Tempo sync in the instrument card (Off, or a loop every 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 beats), and the reactions menu for shapes and for Live.

### Task 9: Checks, performance, the two-hour run, design notes

- **`scripts/verify-map.mjs`, new checks:**
  - scenes switch with keys 1–3, never black, with shared instruments not prepared again
  - a test tone makes a reacting shape's brightness move on the wall
  - injected MIDI: a control change moves an instrument setting, a note switches the scene
  - a Live look follows a signal
- **`scripts/measure-map.mjs --react`:** 16 sound links.
- **`scripts/long-run-map.mjs`:** a three-scene show switching every 30 s for two hours. It records memory and long frames. It runs in the background, with the results written into the design.
