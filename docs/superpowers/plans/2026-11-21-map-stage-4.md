# Map Stage 4 Implementation Plan: placement

**Goal:** Make the picture sit correctly on the real object. Each shape gets a placement (Fit, Pin, Across group, Across stage), the stage's four corners re-pin the whole picture when the projector moves, and each shape gets brightness and tint for evening out surfaces. A grid pinned to an angled box face must look square to the eye, and nudging the projector is fixed with four handles.

**Status:** Approved by Greg on 21 November 2026 and built the same day. Built: placement maths, show version 4, placement / look / stage corner actions, compositor (Pin, Across group, Across stage, brightness, tint, stage corners), the Details panel controls, and in-app checks. Not built: Share with / Make separate, dragging pin corners and stage corners on the stage (they are typed in the Details panel for now), and a separate Across check in the app.

**Spec:** [docs/projection-mapping-design-2026-11-16.md](../../projection-mapping-design-2026-11-16.md): Build order stage 4 row, "Placement: how the picture sits in the shape", "Look, edge and timing" (Look), and "Stage corners" under Lining up.

## Stage 4 decisions (my calls, to confirm)

1. **Pin renders by perspective, not by WebGL.** The compositor keeps Canvas 2D. A pinned picture is drawn as a grid of small triangles, each mapped with an affine `drawImage` transform, which is the standard way to get perspective on Canvas 2D. A 12 × 12 grid is the starting point; the count is tuned against the performance run. The design's stage 2 note says a GPU compositor is only needed for Pin; this decision says it is not, unless the measurement fails. If the measurement fails, Pin moves to WebGL and the decision is revisited.
2. **Pin corners.** A shape with four corners pins to its own corners. A shape with another count pins to a box the operator places (four handles, stored on the shape). Pin corners are stored in stage pixels, the same space as the outline.
3. **Across group and Across stage** lay one picture over the group's bounding box or the whole stage. Each shape shows its part of that picture through its outline. Sharing one source (Share with…) is what makes several shapes show one picture; placement only decides where the picture sits.
4. **Stage corners** are four handles on the operator screen. Dragging one re-pins the whole stage transform (a homography over the stage), applied to every shape together. Shapes keep their positions relative to the stage. The stage corners are stored in the show file and sent to the wall.
5. **Brightness and tint** are per shape. Brightness is a multiplier from 0 to 200 % applied at composite time; tint is a colour blended in at a set strength. Both are cheap enough to apply without a new render.

## Global Constraints

- Studio's loops, seeking and export must not change. Studio and Live tests pass untouched.
- No edit may blank the output. A pin that is refused or degenerate keeps the last valid placement on the wall.
- No mapping jargon on screen. The placement words are Fit, Pin, Across group, Across stage, Brightness, Tint, Stage corners.
- Version 1, 2 and 3 shows open unchanged in meaning. The show file moves to version 4 with defaults for every new field (Fit, no tint, brightness 100 %).
- Limits: keep the stage 3 limits (64 shapes, 32 points a shape, 8 holes, 32 groups, 64 sources, names 40 characters). Pin corners count as four extra handles and are not anchors.
- The largest valid show message still fits `MAP_MESSAGE_LIMIT`, tested.
- Performance holds: `scripts/measure-map.mjs` keeps 120 fps with no frame over 16.7 ms, including a run with Pin on every shape.

## Review Focus

1. **Degenerate pins.** Four pin corners that are collinear, self-crossing or one-pixel wide are refused, and the last valid placement stays on the wall. Tests in the placement maths.
2. **Stage corners move every shape but not the outlines.** The outlines and the picture stay in their stage positions; only the stage-to-wall transform changes. Test: a moved corner shifts the wall output and leaves the saved outlines unchanged.
3. **Across group keeps the picture still when the group moves** unless the operator moves the group's picture. Test: moving a group with Across group shows the same picture through the moved window.
4. **Brightness and tint cost.** Applied at composite time, not by re-rendering the instrument. Test: a counter shows no extra instrument renders for brightness changes.
5. **Triangle grid seams.** Adjacent triangles must not leave hairlines. Test: a flat Pin on a rectangle matches an unpinned Fit to within one pixel at the centre.

## Tasks (outline, to be detailed when approved)

1. **Placement maths** (`src/map/placement.ts`): homography from four pins to a unit square; triangle-grid mapping; degenerate-pin checks. Tests with `node:test`.
2. **Show version 4** (`src/map/show.ts`): placement, pin corners, picture-across mode, brightness, tint, stage corners. Migration from v3 with defaults; validation repairs bad pins to Fit.
3. **Editor actions** (`src/map/editor.ts`): setPlacement, movePin, setBrightness, setTint, setStageCorner, shareSource and makeSeparate (Share with / Make separate). Each undoable.
4. **Compositor** (`src/map/compositor.ts`, `src/map/draw.ts`): Pin by triangle grid; Across group and Across stage by offsetting the source to the group or stage; brightness and tint; stage-corner transform on the output.
5. **Wire** (`src/map/messages.ts`): carry placement and stage corners; keep the size check.
6. **Operator screen** (`src/ui/MapPanel.tsx`, `src/ui/MapDetails.tsx`): placement picker, pin handles, stage corner handles, brightness and tint sliders.
7. **Checks**: unit tests for each task, `npm run test:map` extended with the stage 4 checks, and `scripts/measure-map.mjs` run with Pin on every shape.
8. **Results and known limits** written into the design doc, the same way stage 3 was.
