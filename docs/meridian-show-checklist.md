# Meridian show checklist

Print this on one page. Meridian is alpha until party feedback is in.

## Before doors

- [ ] Mac on power, sleep off, notifications off. Open Form & Field and switch to **Map**.
- [ ] Projector and wall display connected. Check the output is 50 Hz or faster, and that the lines look sharp at full size.
- [ ] Map shapes fit the surfaces. Press **Identify** and the test grid, then check each corner.
- [ ] Audio: choose the DJ input on the Meridian panel. Play the music and see the light follow it. No music yet? Use the test beat.
- [ ] Wall flash limiter is **on** (it is by default). Do not turn it off. It is not a certified photosensitivity test.
- [ ] Press **Blackout**, then bring the picture back, so you know where it is.
- [ ] If using Live: place the camera so it does not see the projection or the lighting rig. Use movement-only mode. Step in front of it and check the shapes follow you.
- [ ] MIDI controller plugged in and its moves learned (if used). Press each once.
- [ ] Look at the frame rate on the show Mac. Close other apps.
- [ ] Single display? Save the display first.
- [ ] Optional: turn on Installation mode so autopilot can run the show.

## During the set

- Default style is Evolve: calm, slow, never resets. Leave it alone most of the time.
- Moves: **N** Next, **H** Hold, **D** Drop, **C** Collapse, **M** Mutate, **R** Reseed. Burst is a button. Keys **1 to 8** recall seed slots.
- Too busy: lower **Density** or **Speed**, or press **H** to hold the shape.
- Too dark or too plain: raise **Heat** or **Weight**, or press **N**.
- Music changes: use **Drop** or **Collapse**. Nothing else is needed.

## If something goes wrong

- **Blackout** first. Take a breath. Bring it back when ready.
- Picture frozen or wrong: press **R** (Reseed). Still wrong: reopen the show file.
- Picture slow: close other apps. The quality governor lowers glow first; it never lowers line sharpness or the limiter.
- Wall window gone or crashed: it does not reopen by itself. Reopen the Map output on the wall display.
- No sound response: check the audio input on the Meridian panel, or use the test beat.
- Live shapes not following people: check the camera is not covered, then switch Live off and on.
- Last resort: quit and reopen the app, open the saved show, press Start on the wall. Fault log (text only) is in the app if you need to tell us what happened.

## After

- [ ] Blackout, then stop the show and quit the app.
- [ ] Save the show if you changed it.
- [ ] Write down what worked and what did not: frame rate, line width, camera position, anything odd.
- [ ] Tell us. Party feedback decides what changes next.
