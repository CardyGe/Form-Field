# Form & Field: re-audit

13 November 2026 · main at `1ff47ef` (0.13.0) · audit only, no code changed

This checks 0.13 against the six preset rules and five stages of the [12 November improvement plan](effects-improvement-plan-2026-11-12.md).

## What was checked

- **Health:** typecheck clean; 102 unit tests and 31 Electron tests pass.
- **Every preset rendered fresh:** 83 presets at phases 0, 1/3 and 2/3 (249 stills at 640×360 on black), plus 57 twelve-frame motion sheets, one per algorithm branch. No render errors. The renders are in `work/audit-2026-11-13/`. `capture.mjs` reruns them.
- **Measured, not just looked at:** `work/audit-2026-11-13/metrics.mjs` scores each preset on four measures:
  - coverage (rule 1)
  - colour strength (rule 2)
  - change between phases at thumbnail size (rule 3)
  - layout similarity within a family, to flag twins (rule 5)

  The results are in `metrics.json`. The numbers flagged candidates; the contact sheets decided.
- **Not covered:** full-speed playback (rule 4, "not in unison", needs eyes on the moving image), 4K performance, and real projector checks.

## Headline

**0.13 is a big step.** The cull, the neon palettes, frame-filling Fractals, Oil layouts, Resonant Sand's mode score and the per-element timing all landed. About **50 of 83 presets now pass every rule from stills**, up from roughly a dozen on 11 November. The strongest work (Surface, Relief, Resonant Sand, Painted Tunnel, Acrylic Pour, Lace Ribbons and the 2D Fractals) is wall-ready.

What's left is concentrated in three places:

1. **Twins survived the cull.** Oil has five recolours of one slide, Living Ink four recolours of one maze, and Graphic about four pairs.
2. **Graphic Patterns is half weak.** Pastel, grey or murky presets, two that barely move, and line work that may be too fine for a projector.
3. **Velvet Currents still fails** after its densify pass. By the plan's own rule, the family should now be cut.

## Follow-up: the cut (13 November, same day)

Step 1 of the next pass is applied to the working tree but not committed. The library goes from **83 to 67 presets**.

- **Velvet Currents retired.** It has been added to `RETIRED_KINDS`, so older projects drop its layers when they open. Removed: its shader, `CurrentRenderer.ts`, its UI, its thumbnails, its export kind, and Silk River and Copper Confluence.
- **Oil:** removed Cellular Lift, Ink Drag, Chromatic Slides and Ember Glass. Acid Slide stays as the one glass-slide look, alongside Cobalt Tide, Acrylic Pour and Lace Ribbons.
- **Living Ink:** removed Cobalt Fingerprints (its stripes were too fine) and Ember Cells (it barely changed).
- **Graphic Patterns:** removed Pillar Wave, Orbital Bloom, Tile Ripple, Woven Vortex, Concentric Halo, Neon Frames and Scalloped Vortex. That leaves 17. Lava Columns is now the first look, so new Graphic layers start from it instead of a pastel look. The variants themselves are all still available.
- **Surface:** removed Deep Bloom. Pop Portal remains the portal look.

Kept, against this audit's twin list:

- **All three Julias.** Each one is the library's only example of something: Lime Julia of the classic variant, Spiral Garden of the spiral variant, and Ember Julia of contour-band colouring. The test suite requires that coverage.
- **Time Terraces.** It is the showcase for the time-displacement finish, and its difference from Neon Strata is in motion, which stills can't judge.
- **Hot Mandala and Midnight Membrane.** Their mode scores step through different figures over the loop, and the family has only three presets.

Tests, scripts and the README were updated to match. This includes the stale README lines noted above (the 0.11 version line, the "no temporal feedback" claim and the Super 8 count).
